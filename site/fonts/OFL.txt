Vendored webfonts — license and attribution
===========================================

The .woff2 files in this directory are unmodified latin and latin-ext subsets,
as served by the Google Fonts API, of five families released under the SIL
Open Font License, Version 1.1.

The full license text is reproduced below rather than linked. The OFL FAQ
tolerates a bare link only for fonts embedded in a document or bundled inside
a program; these are served as standalone files, so condition 2 applies in
full — each copy must carry the copyright notice and this license.

the Google Fonts API, so the Reserved Font Name condition is not engaged.
